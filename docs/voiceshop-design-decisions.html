Portable creative brief · 30 July 2026

VoiceShop

Product and interaction decisions for agents creating additional marketing-site concepts. This brief intentionally excludes existing visual directions so new work can remain genuinely independent.

Product and audience

  • The customer-facing product name is VoiceShop.
  • VoiceShop is a voice-first AI sales agent for Shopify storefronts.
  • The primary audience is ecommerce founders, operators, and growth teams evaluating a sales-focused shopping assistant.
  • The sibling digital-salesman-app repository is the source of truth for product capabilities; it is not the public product name.

Verified product story

  • Understands the storefront page a shopper is viewing.
  • Searches the published product catalog for broad discovery and specific products.
  • Presents products in the conversation and changes the main storefront surface.
  • Uses current catalog, price, inventory, variant, and cart context rather than inventing products or availability.
  • Can open products and initiate validated add-to-cart or remove-from-cart actions.
Positioning distinction The shopper speaks and the storefront responds. Present VoiceShop as an active sales layer, not a generic support chatbot.

Required deliverable

  • Create five complete and visually independent landing pages.
  • Each page includes a hero, concrete product proof, workflow or product depth, and a final waitlist conversion.
  • A persistent, accessible control cycles through the five designs.
  • The active design is represented in the URL so it survives refresh and can be shared.
  • Shared logic is acceptable, but layouts must be independent components rather than one template with five themes.

Conversion behavior

  • The primary CTA is Join the waitlist.
  • Waitlist forms are visual prototypes only: no network request, database, service, analytics event, or success claim.
  • Submitting must not navigate away or imply that data was stored.

Visual constraints

  • The dominant page theme must be light, not dark.
  • Do not use an editorial or magazine-style art direction.
  • Treat the first viewport as a strong product poster with one dominant visual anchor and unmistakable VoiceShop branding.
  • Avoid generic SaaS card grids, decorative dashboard mosaics, excessive pills, filler copy, and theme-only recolors.
  • Use no more than two typefaces per concept.
  • Use Loomi Shopping Agent by Bloomreach and Rep AI Sales Agent as market-caliber references for product clarity and guided-commerce storytelling, never as layouts to copy.

Mobile, accessibility, and motion

  • Compose each concept for mobile deliberately; do not merely shrink the desktop page.
  • Budget full-height sections with 100svh, including persistent controls.
  • Use responsive media, readable line lengths, 44px minimum tap targets, and 16px form text to avoid iOS input zoom.
  • Keep the design-cycle control thumb-accessible and clear of primary content.
  • Maintain semantic landmarks, visible focus states, sufficient contrast, labelled inputs, and keyboard operability.
  • Include purposeful entrance, scroll, and interaction motion while honoring prefers-reduced-motion.

Do not invent

  • No conversion, revenue, ROI, latency, or adoption metrics.
  • No customer logos, testimonials, awards, or partner claims.
  • No waitlist confirmation or claim that an email was saved.
  • No unverified checkout or payment completion claims.

Quality gate

  • Verify all five designs at desktop and mobile widths.
  • Verify cycling, direct URL selection, refresh, and wraparound.
  • Verify keyboard focus and reduced-motion behavior.
  • Run lint, TypeScript checking, and the production build.

Competitive references: Loomi Shopping Agent and Rep AI Sales Agent.
